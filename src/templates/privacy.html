Privacy & Security

How we protect your data

Encrypted at rest

Your conversations and personal data are encrypted using AES-128 (Fernet).

Data isolation

Your data is isolated at the database level with row-level security. No user can access another user's data.

No data selling

We never sell, share, or monetize your learning data. Your conversations exist to help you learn, nothing else.

Open source

Habla Hermano is fully open source. You can inspect every line of code that handles your data.
